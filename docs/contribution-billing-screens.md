# Contribution Billing screens

Menu path: **Contribution Billing** (legacy `mdiMain.frm`): Generate Invoice, Generate Invoice (Endorsement), MCO Reinstatement, Refund Listing and Receipt MCO/Others (the last is the Finance > Invoice Receipt (Outstanding) screen, already built). The sidebar has a Contribution Billing module (rail icon, `/contribution-billing`); every visible item has a screen. The desktop's hidden items (MCO Correction, Date Correction, Invoice Manual Upd, Receipt MCO/Others (Manual), Foreign Workers, MCO Invoice (ASCII)) are not Required and are not built.

## Generate Invoice

Reads and writes (`sql/main/580_contribution_generate_invoice.sql`, `ContributionBilling/GenerateInvoice/GenerateInvoice.cs`, `GenerateInvoicePage.tsx`). The MCO fees of a bordx batch or range of a payor, or of an invoice (`VIEW_MCO_LISTING_All2`), and the MCO invoice for them. It is MCO Reinstatement's bigger sibling and takes its invoice numbers from the same counters.

- **Search:** **MCO Criteria** (Bordereaux Batch: payor, bordx date and batch; Bordereaux Range: payor and a range of bordx dates; Invoice Number), the checks and messages of the desktop in its order (`Please Enter MCO Criteria!`, `Please Enter Leaflet Version !`, `Please Enter Payor!`, `Please Enter Bordereaux Date!`, `Please Enter Batch No!`, `Please Enter Bordereaux Date From!` / `To!`, `Please Enter Invoice No!`, `Please Enter Group Company!`); nothing found is `Record not found !`. A batch or range lists only the members that **have no invoice yet**; an invoice lists its members. **Group By** (the desktop's By Payor / By Department / By Group Company; the last one needs a group company, chosen from the payor's), and **Principal only** (the desktop's three "- Principal" criteria, which only changed the report). The list has the 18 columns of the desktop; **Total Principal** and **Total Supp** count them. `MCO Fee is Empty!` warns of a member with no fee; more than one invoice in the list blocks Print and Preview (`Different Invoice Found in Same Batch or Range, Please Search by Invoice No ! `).
- **Print and Preview:** both need a **Report Type** (Invoice or Fee Listing; `Please Select Report Type !`), and Fee Listing offers **Listing Versions** (MCO Listing, MCO Listing With Premium). **Print** gives the members one new invoice number and, for each member or covered person, in one transaction: the invoice number, date and user on the member (`MBMOthers`, cover 00) or the covered person (`MBMCoveredPersons`), the member's `MCOPending` row to status B (HISMaintenance), and a row in `MBMMCOInvoice` (plan, insurance code, dates, adult / child, basic MCO and billed amount). The server refuses Print for members that are billed already (the desktop switched the button off); **Preview** writes nothing and is how an invoice is printed again. The screen asks before a number is given out (the desktop did not).
- **The number and who is billed:** the same `MBMMCOINVQ` counters as MCO Reinstatement (the shared S counter, or the payor's own), taken under an update lock. A payor whose `BillingPartyStatus` is C (WK) is billed through its **broker** (the `Broker` table, prefix S); the others to themselves (`PAY.MCOInvoicePrefix`, S when it has none).
- **The files:** the **invoice** (the payor or broker's name, address, contact, telephone and fax, the invoice number and date, one line per group, insurance code, plan and adult / child with the principals, members, basic MCO and MCO fee, and the totals) and the **fee listing** (one line per member and covered person, with the premium in the premium version, and the totals) are CSV files; the desktop printed six Crystal reports (`MCOInvoice`, `MCOInvoice_Dept`, `MCOListing_WithoutPremium`, `_Dept`, `MCOListing_Premium`, `_Dept`) that are not in the source, so their layouts are not reproduced and the invoice lines are grouped as stated. The copy into `MCOInvoicePluto` the Crystal reports read is not needed.
- **Data:** payor WK, bordx 2021-04-30 batch 2 (12 members, billed through the broker) is the test batch; invoice S34161 (AN, 5 members) the billed one. Batch AH 2021-07-30 / 1 has 3,185 members with no invoice.
- **Tests:** `GenerateInvoiceTests.cs` (validators, the print flow and its refusals against a fake repository; the search, the payor and broker, the group companies and the stamping in a rolled-back session, checked with checksums); `generateInvoiceRules.test.ts` (14 of the 20 in the folder); `e2e/contribution-generate-invoice.mjs` (39 checks).

## Generate Invoice (Endorsement) (rebuilt: no source)

`mdiMain.frm` opens `frmMCOFeesListingEndt` (`FeeListingEndt_Click`, position 8, the one Generate Invoice checks), and the form is not in the source tree; there is no procedure or view of its own either. The screen is rebuilt as **Generate Invoice with one more MCO Criteria, Endorsement Range** (`McoInvoice_Search @endtFrom, @endtTo`; the same page with `endorsement`, the same API, invoice numbers, payor / broker, stamping, invoice and fee listing):

- **Who is billed:** the members of the payor with **no invoice yet** whose endorsement bordx date (`MBMTwo.MBMENDtBordxDate`) is in the range, with their covered persons with no invoice, from `VIEW_MCO_LISTING_All2`. This is how the desktop's other endorsement invoice chose them (`SP_MCOASCII_Endorsmnt`, the hidden Zurich MCO Invoice (ASCII): unbilled members, `MBMEndtBordxDate` from / to). Invoice Number finds a billed endorsement invoice again (Preview reprints it).
- **No endorsement batch:** `MBM.MBMENDtBatchNo` is empty on all 136,608 endorsed members, so there is no Bordereaux Batch criteria on this screen.
- **Data:** BS, endorsement bordx 2021-08-05 (7 members, RM 210 of MCO fees) is a test range; 5,647 of the endorsed members are billed already and are left out.
- **Assumptions for the Contribution Billing users to confirm:** that the endorsement invoice bills the member's whole MCO fee as it stands (`MBMMCOFee`, as Generate Invoice does) and not a difference between the old and the new plan; that every unbilled covered person of an endorsed member is billed with it, whatever its own bordx date (17% of them have another date); and that the invoice takes the same number series as Generate Invoice.
- **Decision (2026-11-10): kept as built.** Checked in the browser: for an invoiced payor (AX, endorsement bordx 5 to 6 Aug 2021, 16 rows) the invoice also takes the endorsed members' rows whose ordinary bordx was never invoiced, at the full fee, and rows with no MCO fee; for a payor that is never MCO-invoiced (BS) it takes whole families. Billing only the rows that came in with the endorsement was considered and not chosen; a row billed here is stamped and so is not billed again by Generate Invoice.
- **Tests:** `GenerateInvoiceTests.cs` (the endorsement checks, and the search against the database: every row unbilled, of the payor, of a member endorsed in the range); `generateInvoiceRules.test.ts` (the checks and the query).

## MCO Reinstatement

Reads and writes (`sql/main/530_contribution_mco_reinstatement.sql`, `ContributionBilling/McoReinstatement/McoReinstatement.cs`, `ContributionBilling/GenerateInvoice/GenerateInvoice.cs`, `McoReinstatementPage.tsx`, `generateInvoiceRules.ts`, `GenerateInvoicePage.tsx`). The MCO fees of members whose cover was reinstated (`MBMReinstatement`, listed through `VIEW_MBM_MCOReinstatement`), and the invoice for them.

- **Search:** **MCO Criteria**: Bordx Date / Batch No (with the **Payor**, the endorsement bordx date and the batch) or Invoice No. The checks and messages are the desktop's, in its order: `Please Enter MCO Criteria!`, `Please Enter Payor!`, `Please Enter Bordereaux Date!`, `Please Enter Batch No!` (the batch has to be a number), `Please Enter Invoice No!`; nothing found is `Record not found !`. The list has the desktop's 13 columns: a principal (relation P, Adult) and its covered persons; the fee is rounded to a whole number. **Total Principal** and **Total Supp** count them.
- **Print and Preview:** both need a **Report Type** (`Please Select Report Type !`): the invoice or the listing of the members. The server searches again, so the document is of what is in the database. **Print** gives the members one new invoice number when none of them has one, and writes the invoice or listing; a batch that has its invoice is printed again with the same number and nothing is written. **Preview** writes nothing (the desktop's print preview, which did not give out a number). Because giving out a number cannot be undone, the screen asks first (`Printing gives these members a new invoice number. Do you want to continue?`): the desktop did not.
- **The invoice number** is taken from `MBMMCOINVQ` as the desktop did it: a payor whose prefix (`PAY.MCOInvoicePrefix`, S when it has none) is S shares the counter of the first row of the table (the one with no payor: `S34163` and counting), a payor with another prefix (XP: P, XW: F) has its own row, created at 00001 when it has none; the number has five digits, and the row's own prefix is the one used. The row is **locked** until the transaction ends, so two people printing at the same time cannot get the same number (the desktop read and updated an optimistic recordset). Each line is stamped (invoice number, date, user) in the same transaction; only the lines of the batch are stamped (the desktop stamped every row of the member, whatever its batch).
- **A batch with different or partial invoices is refused** (`Different Invoice Found in Same Batch or Range, Please Search by Invoice No ! `). The desktop had this check but never set the flag it tested and looked only at the first member to see whether an invoice existed; the web looks at all of them. One invoice can cover several batches (S33290 covers 2020-09-07 to 2020-09-11), which is why an invoice number search is offered.
- **The files:** the **invoice** (payor name, address, contact, telephone and fax, invoice number and date, one line per bordx batch, insurance code and adult / child with the principals, the covered persons and the rounded fee, and the total; `MCOInvoiceTemplate.xls` in the desktop) and the **listing** (batch, invoice and date at the top, one line per member with policy, name, relation, IC, group company, days insured and the rounded fee, and the total; `MCOListingTemplate.xls`) are CSV files. The category summary block at the bottom of the listing template is not repeated (the invoice lines carry it).
- **Data:** 62,561 reinstatement lines, 45,064 of them invoiced; 12 rows in `MBMMCOINVQ`. Batch AH 2021-07-26 / 1 has 1 principal and 3 covered persons and no invoice.
- **Tests:** `McoReinstatementTests.cs` (the validators, the allocation and refusal flows against a fake repository, and the search, the invoice lines, the shared and own counters, the stamping and the rollback against the database, checked with checksums); `mcoReinstatementRules.test.ts` (7); `e2e/contribution-mco-reinstatement.mjs` (36 checks; Preview is a real call that writes nothing, the Print that would give a number is intercepted).

## Refund Listing

Read only (`sql/main/520_contribution_refund_listing.sql`, `ContributionBilling/RefundListing/RefundListing.cs`, `ContributionBilling/McoReinstatement/McoReinstatement.cs`, `RefundListingPage.tsx`). Lists the MCO fees refunded to a payor's cancelled or endorsed members, from `VIEW_MBM_Refund_Search` (the principals) and `View_MBM_Supplementary_Refund` (their dependants).

- **Criteria:** **Date Selection** (Endt. Eff Date, the endorsement effective date, or Endt. Bordx Date, the endorsement bordx date; the date boxes are named after it), **Payor**, the date range (a blank date is not applied) and **Print Status** (N - No: members without a credit note; Y - Yes: members with one). `Please Select Date Selection!` then `Please Select Payor!` as in the desktop. A payor that is not there is not found.
- **The list:** one line per principal, in member number order, with the desktop's 22 columns; the member's status follows the name ("(Cancelled)", "(Pending)"). A **family or household principal** (insurance code F or H) is followed by its supplementary members, which carry the payor and date criteria but not the print status, as in the desktop (it looked them up one principal at a time; the web reads them all at once and matches them by member number). A **Type** column says which is which.
- **Arithmetic, as the desktop coded it** (the business should confirm it is what is wanted):
  - **MCO fees Refund (Min.):** with a utilised fee at or below the minimum (50 for a family or household principal, 25 for the others and for the supplementary members), the fee less the minimum; above it, the **utilised fee itself** (not the fee less the utilised fee); 0 without a utilised fee. A missing fee counts as 0.
  - **Days Utilised:** for a payor whose `MBMCancelStatus` is Y (the screen's `MBMStatus` flag, set when the payor is chosen) a value below zero shows as 0; for the other payors the value as it is. This is the only difference between the desktop's two principal listings (`MCORefundListing` and `MCORefundListing2`, case 1 and case 2 of `MBMRefundListing`); the one filter on the cancelled status and 30 days that the first one seems to have is commented out in the source.
  - **Totals:** **Total Principal** and **Total Supp** count the lines; **Total MCO Refund** adds the principals' MCO fees refund. The desktop had a second total, "Total MCO Refund (Min.)", which added the same column and so always showed the same figure; it is not repeated. Supplementary refunds are counted, not added.
- **Print** writes the list to CSV (the desktop wrote an Excel sheet). **Records** over 5,000 principals are cut and the screen says so.
- **Not migrated: Print CR Note.** It generates a credit note number (`GenerateMCOCreditNo`), writes it to the members (`UpdateCreditNote` updates `MBMOthers` and `MBMCoveredPersonsTwo`) and prints the credit note through Excel. How credit and debit notes are numbered is an open business question (see [remaining-work.md](remaining-work.md)), so the button is left out until it is answered; the Credit Note and Credit Date columns still show the notes that were printed in the desktop.
- **Data:** the principal view has 964,608 rows (481,368 for payor DC), most with no endorsement, so a search without dates for a big payor is cut at 5,000. Payors with endorsements in the data: AX (92, all family or household), AH (78), ST (48), AY (32), WK (19), BS (17). The supplementary view has 169 rows. No credit note has been printed for them.
- **Tests:** `RefundListingTests.cs` (validator, arithmetic, service, and each criterion against direct DISTINCT counts on the views); `refundListingRules.test.ts` (5); `e2e/contribution-refund-listing.mjs` (29 checks).
