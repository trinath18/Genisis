## MCO Reinstatement

Reads and writes (`sql/main/530_contribution_mco_reinstatement.sql`, `ContributionBilling/McoReinstatement/McoReinstatement.cs`, `McoReinstatementPage.tsx`). The MCO fees of members whose cover was reinstated (`MBMReinstatement`, listed through `VIEW_MBM_MCOReinstatement`), and the invoice for them.

- **Search:** **MCO Criteria**: Bordx Date / Batch No (with the **Payor**, the endorsement bordx date and the batch) or Invoice No. The checks and messages are the desktop's, in its order: `Please Enter MCO Criteria!`, `Please Enter Payor!`, `Please Enter Bordereaux Date!`, `Please Enter Batch No!` (the batch has to be a number), `Please Enter Invoice No!`; nothing found is `Record not found !`. The list has the desktop's 13 columns: a principal (relation P, Adult) and its covered persons; the fee is rounded to a whole number. **Total Principal** and **Total Supp** count them.
- **Print and Preview:** both need a **Report Type** (`Please Select Report Type !`): the invoice or the listing of the members. The server searches again, so the document is of what is in the database. **Print** gives the members one new invoice number when none of them has one, and writes the invoice or listing; a batch that has its invoice is printed again with the same number and nothing is written. **Preview** writes nothing (the desktop's print preview, which did not give out a number). Because giving out a number cannot be undone, the screen asks first (`Printing gives these members a new invoice number. Do you want to continue?`): the desktop did not.
- **The invoice number** is taken from `MBMMCOINVQ` as the desktop did it: a payor whose prefix (`PAY.MCOInvoicePrefix`, S when it has none) is S shares the counter of the first row of the table (the one with no payor: `S34163` and counting), a payor with another prefix (XP: P, XW: F) has its own row, created at 00001 when it has none; the number has five digits, and the row's own prefix is the one used. The row is **locked** until the transaction ends, so two people printing at the same time cannot get the same number (the desktop read and updated an optimistic recordset). Each line is stamped (invoice number, date, user) in the same transaction; only the lines of the batch are stamped (the desktop stamped every row of the member, whatever its batch).
- **A batch with different or partial invoices is refused** (`Different Invoice Found in Same Batch or Range, Please Search by Invoice No ! `). The desktop had this check but never set the flag it tested and looked only at the first member to see whether an invoice existed; the web looks at all of them. One invoice can cover several batches (S33290 covers 2020-09-07 to 2020-09-11), which is why an invoice number search is offered.
- **The files:** the **invoice** (payor name, address, contact, telephone and fax, invoice number and date, one line per bordx batch, insurance code and adult / child with the principals, the covered persons and the rounded fee, and the total; `MCOInvoiceTemplate.xls` in the desktop) and the **listing** (batch, invoice and date at the top, one line per member with policy, name, relation, IC, group company, days insured and the rounded fee, and the total; `MCOListingTemplate.xls`) are CSV files. The category summary block at the bottom of the listing template is not repeated (the invoice lines carry it).
- **Data:** 62,561 reinstatement lines, 45,064 of them invoiced; 12 rows in `MBMMCOINVQ`. Batch AH 2021-07-26 / 1 has 1 principal and 3 covered persons and no invoice.
- **Tests:** `McoReinstatementTests.cs` (the validators, the allocation and refusal flows against a fake repository, and the search, the invoice lines, the shared and own counters, the stamping and the rollback against the database, checked with checksums); `mcoReinstatementRules.test.ts` (7); `e2e/contribution-mco-reinstatement.mjs` (36 checks; Preview is a real call that writes nothing, the Print that would give a number is intercepted).

## Refund Listing

Read only (`sql/main/520_contribution_refund_listing.sql`, `ContributionBilling/RefundListing/RefundListing.cs`, `ContributionBilling/McoReinstatement/McoReinstatement.cs`, `RefundListingPage.tsx`). Lists the MCO fees refunded to a payor's cancelled or endorsed members, from `VIEW_MBM_Refund_Search` (the principals) and `View_MBM_Supplementary_Refund` (their dependants).

- **Criteria:** **Date Selection** (Endt. Eff Date, the endorsement effective date, or Endt. Bordx Date, the endorsement bordx date; the date boxes are named after it), **Payor**, the date range (a blank date is not applied) and **Print Status** (N - No: members without a credit note; Y - Yes: members with one). `Please Select Date Selection!` then `Please Select Payor!` as in the desktop. A payor that is not there is not found.
- **The list:** one line per principal, in member number order, with the desktop's 22 columns; the member's status follows the name ("(Cancelled)", "(Pending)"). A **family or household principal** (insurance code F or H) is followed by its supplementary members, which carry the payor and date criteria but not the print status, as in the desktop (it looked them up one principal at a time; the web reads them all at once and matches them by member number). A **Type** column says which is which.
- **Arithmetic, as the desktop coded it** (the business should confirm it is what is wanted):
  - **MCO fees Refund (Min.):** with a utilised fee at or below the minimum (50 for a family or household principal, 25 for the others and for the supplementary members), the fee less the minimum; above it, the **utilised fee itself** (not the fee less the utilised fee); 0 without a utilised fee. A missing fee counts as 0.
  - **Days Utilised:** for a payor whose `MBMCancelStatus` is Y (the screen's `MBMStatus` flag, set when the payor is chosen) a value below zero shows as 0; for the other payors the value as it is. This is the only difference between the desktop's two principal listings (`MCORefundListing` and `MCORefundListing2`, case 1 and case 2 of `MBMRefundListing`); the one filter on the cancelled status and 30 days that the first one seems to have is commented out in the source.
  - **Totals:** **Total Principal** and **Total Supp** count the lines; **Total MCO Refund** adds the principals' MCO fees refund. The desktop had a second total, "Total MCO Refund (Min.)", which added the same column and so always showed the same figure; it is not repeated. Supplementary refunds are counted, not added.
- **Print** writes the list to CSV (the desktop wrote an Excel sheet). **Records** over 5,000 principals are cut and the screen says so.
- **Not migrated: Print CR Note.** It generates a credit note number (`GenerateMCOCreditNo`), writes it to the members (`UpdateCreditNote` updates `MBMOthers` and `MBMCoveredPersonsTwo`) and prints the credit note through Excel. How credit and debit notes are numbered is an open business question (see [remaining-work.md](remaining-work.md)), so the button is left out until it is answered; the Credit Note and Credit Date columns still show the notes that were printed in the desktop.
- **Data:** the principal view has 964,608 rows (481,368 for payor DC), most with no endorsement, so a search without dates for a big payor is cut at 5,000. Payors with endorsements in the data: AX (92, all family or household), AH (78), ST (48), AY (32), WK (19), BS (17). The supplementary view has 169 rows. No credit note has been printed for them.
- **Tests:** `RefundListingTests.cs` (validator, arithmetic, service, and each criterion against direct DISTINCT counts on the views); `refundListingRules.test.ts` (5); `e2e/contribution-refund-listing.mjs` (29 checks).
