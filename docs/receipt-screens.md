Menu path: **Receipt** (legacy `mdiMain.frm`, top menu `mnuReceiptModule`: From Member, Others, Payor, Payor (Manual), Receipt Tracking). The business marked From Member, Others, Payor and Receipt Tracking Required. Receipt Tracking and From Member are built; the three entry forms are in [remaining-work.md](remaining-work.md) and are shown disabled in the Receipt menu. The sidebar has a Receipt module (rail icon, `/receipt`).

## From Member

Reads and writes (`sql/main/590_receipt_from_member.sql`, `Receipt/Member/ReceiptMember.cs`, `ReceiptMemberPage.tsx`, `receiptMemberRules.ts`). The debit notes raised to members who owe money (`VIEW_ReceiptFrMBM` where the claim's amount payable by MEDIX is zero or negative and the DR number starts with D: 2,666 of them, 2,167 unpaid) and the receipts of what they paid back (`ReceiptFrMBM`, 21,812 rows: 21,580 full, 209 partial; `ContraAllocation`, 2,745).

- **Search:** **Use Scan** (on by default) takes one worksheet number (`Please Enter Worksheet No`), adds its debit notes to the list and clears the box for the next scan; a worksheet already in the list is `Data has been uploaded!`. Without Use Scan the case number range replaces the list. The other criteria are the desktop's: member number and name (contains), payor, **Payment Status** (0 All, 1 Paid, 2 Unpaid, 3 Partial Paid, 4 Paid & Partial Paid, 5 Unpaid & Partial Paid), receipt number, receipt date, DR number, DR amount, accounting period month and year, each with its "is empty" switch. Nothing found is `No record found!`. The list has the 21 columns of the desktop after the tick box; **Received Amt**, **Checked Amt** and **Records** count them. The health-type box of the desktop searched nothing and is not offered; the scan of a `LO` code (`SearchForm`) is not migrated.
- **Amounts:** Amount is the debit note as a positive; OS Amt is the amount less this receipt row's amount for a partial payment, nothing for a full one and the whole amount for none (the desktop's rule, so a claim with several partial rows shows each row against the whole amount).
- **Ticking** (`ProcessCheckedItem`): a row with no receipt becomes Full for the whole amount; a partial row becomes Full for what is outstanding; a row paid in full is refused (`Payment has been made!`) for a user whose `USR.AuthorisedStatus` is N (614 users) and taken again for the whole amount for an authorised one (99 users). Unticking puts the row back and clears what was staged.
- **Update Case** (`CmdAdd_Click`): for the selected ticked claim, the checks in the desktop's order (a contra case needs its version and the version its case: `Invalid Contra Case..., Please Keyin Contra Case No. or Contra Version `; a contra case needs its amount: `Please Keyin Contra Case Amount `; the amount paid cannot exceed the amount: `Invalid Amount Paid ! `), "Update <claim> ?", then an **Amt Paid** replaces the amount receiving (Full when it is the amount or what was being received, otherwise Partial with the difference outstanding) and bank charge, contra case, gain/loss, misc. difference, discount, write off, Medix receipt and remarks are put on the row. The **Amt Paid** column is saved as the row had it (the desktop never changed it; it is 0 in all but 7 of the 21,812 rows).
- **Save** (`cmdSave_Click`): receipt number, date, amount and cheque number, in that order (`Please Enter Receipt No` ...), "Do you want to update the record?", then for each ticked claim, in one transaction: a user who is not authorised updates the receipt row of the same claim and receipt number, or adds one; an authorised user always adds a row (`UpdateRecord`); a full payment also marks the claim's first receipt row F; a contra case updates or adds the claim's `ContraAllocation` row with this receipt number, the amount received and the contra case, version and amount (`UpdateContra`). The account period is the receipt date's month and year until it is changed. `Record updated...`. **Differences:** a claim must be a member debit note (the screen only lists those); a user who is not authorised is refused when the claim has any row paid in full (the desktop stopped only the row clicked, so a partial row of a claim already paid in full could be received again); nothing ticked says `Please tick the claims to be received.` (the desktop did nothing).
- **Edit / Update** (`CmdEdit`, `lstReceiptMBM_Click`, `CmdUpdate_Click`): Edit, then a click on a receipt row, loads it into the boxes (`Payment hasn't been made!` for a row with no receipt); Update checks the list, receipt number, the date (`Invalid Receipt Date!`), cheque number and cheque amount, asks, and changes the row (an empty number is stored as NULL) and its contra allocation (found by the receipt number the row had). **Difference:** the desktop added a receipt row with no claim number when the row had no receipt; a row that does not exist is not changed.
- **Print OR** (`cmdPrintPV_Click`): the official receipt (`View_ReceiptFrMBMRec2`: receipt number, member, date, amount, cheque number, and the patient, DR number and claim of each row) as a CSV; the desktop filled the OR-MBMS (Sihat) or OR-MBMN Excel template, which is not in the source. **Print to File** writes the list.
- **Differences in the search:** the DR amount boxes compare the amount as the list shows it (positive); the desktop compared the negative stored value with a comma-formatted text, which could never match; the receipt number "to" box is applied on its own (the desktop tested the "from" box before applying it).
- **Tests:** `ReceiptMemberTests.cs` (validators, the ticking rules' server side and the save flow against a fake repository; the search, the user's status, the save with both user kinds, the contra allocation, the update and the official receipt in rolled-back sessions, checked with checksums); `receiptMemberRules.test.ts` (22); `e2e/receipt-from-member.mjs` (60 checks; writes intercepted).

## Receipt Tracking

Read only (`sql/main/500_receipt_tracking.sql`, `Receipt/Tracking/ReceiptTracking.cs`). Follows the receipts that came in from **members**, **payors** and **MNRB**. The desktop had twelve lists (`ReceiptTracking` cases 1 to 12 in HISPayment, called with a text WHERE fragment); there are two choices over one search, the kind of receipt (**Receipts from**: Member, which the form opens on, Payor, MNRB) and the layout (**Reports Type**):

| Reports Type | What it lists | Desktop cases (member, payor, MNRB) |
|---|---|---|
| **By Claim** (the form opens on it) | one line per receipt line: receipt number and date, amount, payor, member number and name, claim (case-version), debit note, remarks, accounting period (member list) or bordx, worksheet, debit note, remarks and accounting period (payor and MNRB lists) | 1, 4, 7 |
| **By Bordx** | each bordx, its date, the number of cases and the amount received | 2, 5, 8 |
| **By Receipt No** | each receipt, its date, the number of cases and the amount received | 3, 6, 9 |
| **By Payor** | each payor, the number of receipts and the amount received | 10, 11, 12 |

- **Criteria:** health type (S - Sihat, N - Non-Sihat), payor, bordx number range, receipt number range, receipt date range, and, for By Claim only, member number and name (contains), claim number range, accounting period month and year ranges, debit note range and amount range. A summary only uses the health type, payor, receipt date and the bordx and receipt number ranges; the other boxes are hidden, as the desktop ignored them. A blank criterion is not applied; text ranges compare as text (the desktop's month list was 1 to 12 and its year list 1999 to 3000, typed here).
- **Amount column:** the member list shows the receipt amount; the payor and MNRB lists show the **cheque amount** (`CheckAmt`), as the desktop listed it. The amount criterion is on the receipt amount in all three.
- **Messages:** `No record found!`, `Report cannot be printed without any record.`; **Print to File** exports the list on the screen to CSV. **Records** and **Total Amt** count the lines listed (and add the amount column).
- **Server:** the list is cut at 5,000 rows and says so (the member receipts are 21,812). The summary counts `COUNT(BordxNo)` / `COUNT(ReceiptNo)` as the desktop did, so a line with no receipt number counts 0 cases.
- **Differences:** the amount boxes are checked as numbers on the screen (the desktop said "Amount From is not Integer! It would be bypassed!" for a bad From box and silently ignored a bad To box). The code that built the criteria three times (once per layout) and the 12 listing routines are gone.
- **Data:** member receipts 21,812; payor receipts 23,885; MNRB receipts 10 (its summary view is empty). Summary views: 2,810, 15,826 and 0 lines.
- **Tests:** `ReceiptTrackingTests.cs` (validator, service, and each view and criterion against direct counts and sums); `e2e/receipt-tracking.mjs` (24 checks).
